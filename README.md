# Luxe Miami Aesthetics

Static site for a Palm Beach office planned for 2027 and the Coral Gables flagship, coming 2028. Neither is open. Not yet offering treatments. A franchise is not being offered, and The Atelier membership is not being offered. The site root is the public folder.

The homepage follows a medspa landing rhythm — light header, full-bleed hero, perk band, treatment cards, a standards carousel, one location, an about split, a future-studio block, waitlist, and a dark footer — written entirely for Luxe.

## Pages

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Homepage. Guest waitlist and future-studio interest both post to the Formspree placeholder. |
| `confirmed.html` | No-JavaScript confirmation for the guest waitlist. |
| `interest.html` | No-JavaScript confirmation for a future-studio note. It is not a franchise offer. |

With JavaScript, a successful signup stays on the homepage and shows the matching confirmation. Both forms still post to `https://formspree.io/f/your-form-id` until a real Formspree id is set.

## Photography

The homepage frame is the static black-and-white portrait `spa/hero-bw.jpg`. It is also the Open Graph image. `hero.mp4`, `night.mp4`, `hero-poster.jpg`, and `night-poster.jpg` stay in the repo and are not used on the hero or on Own a Luxe. The Own a Luxe band is `spa/own-palm-beach.jpg`, an original photograph made for Luxe: a quiet Palm Beach clinic exterior, cream stucco, a burgundy awning, antique-gold lanterns, and royal palms. It is a still for the future office at 240 Royal Palm Wy. It is not a photograph of that address, and it is not an asset from the reference medspa.

What’s coming uses nine square black-and-white macros in `spa/`. About uses `spa/about-lifestyle.jpg`, a warm lifestyle portrait. That photo is not Mateo. `founder.jpg` stays in the repo. The Coral Gables card uses `spa/location-interior.jpg`, a burgundy tiled corridor in a 3:4 frame. The Palm Beach card uses an original sepia Florida map, `spa/florida-map.jpg`, and an original line drawing, `spa/palm-beach-line.jpg`, on a paper card marked Opening 2027. These were made for Luxe. They are not pictures of either address, and they are not assets from the reference medspa.

The header and footer use `logo.png` (white script “luxe”, darkened with CSS on the light ground) and the words “Miami Aesthetics” in small type underneath. Page colors follow the light palette sampled from the reference site: `#fefbf5`, `#fdf7ee`, `#ffffff`, `#000000`, `#878787`, `#dddddd`, `#ede1d5`, `#c2a68d`, `#b27860`, and buttons `#000000` / `#fefbf5` with hover `#633631`.

What’s coming is nine planned services. The squares do not carry a “not offered yet” label. The section copy still says they are planned for after licensure and are not available now. The Atelier is a future membership with no fee and no rate. Hours say neither studio is open. Palm Beach, 240 Royal Palm Wy, is opening 2027. Coral Gables, Unit 110, remains the flagship, coming 2028. Franchise copy is interest only. The Palm Beach office is not for sale. No fees or disclosure.
