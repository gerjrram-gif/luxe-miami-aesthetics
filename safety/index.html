Safety

Luxe Miami Aesthetics — Coral Gables, FL
Practice opening 2028. This page describes how we intend to approach safety when clinical services begin. We are not offering injections or medical treatments today.

Our approach

Quality over quantity. Treatments will be performed only by appropriately licensed clinicians under Florida rules, with physician collaboration as required. Medical Director: to be named.

Before any treatment (when open)

  • Full medical history and candidacy review
  • Discussion of goals, alternatives, risks, and benefits
  • Written informed consent
  • Time for questions — no pressure

Injections and aesthetic procedures

Injectable and facial treatments carry risks that can include bruising, swelling, redness, asymmetry, infection, vascular events, and rare serious complications. Your provider will review risks specific to your plan. Results vary. No outcome is guaranteed.

Aftercare

You will receive written aftercare instructions. Follow them. Contact us with concerns. Seek emergency care (911) for severe pain, trouble breathing, vision changes, or signs of stroke / anaphylaxis.

Consent and photos

Clinical consent is separate from marketing photo consent. We never publish identifiable client photos without written marketing consent. See the Photo Authenticity Policy.

Emergency

This Site cannot provide emergency care. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department for urgent medical problems.
