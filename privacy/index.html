Privacy

A note, held quietly.

Luxe Miami Aesthetics is taking shape. This page says what happens when you write to us from the site. Nothing here is for sale. Updated November 4, 2026.

Who we are

Luxe Miami Aesthetics. The only contact on this site is info@luxemiamiaesthetics.com. The practice is not open, and no treatment is offered yet.

What you may send

The forms ask only for what the note needs:

  • Guest waitlist: name, a shipping address for a complimentary gift, email, and an Instagram handle, plus confirmation that you follow @luxemiamiaesthetics.
  • LX-06 Gratitude waitlist: name, a shipping address for a complimentary gift, email, and an Instagram handle, plus confirmation that you follow @luxemiamiaesthetics, whether you want word about the set or about candles later, consent to be contacted, and a confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • A product “Notify me” note: your email, and which piece it is about.
  • A future-studio note: name, email, a city you have in mind, and anything you choose to add.

Please don’t send medical details. We are not taking them on this site.

Why we ask

To reply about the waitlist, a piece still taking shape, or a future studio, and to send a complimentary gift to a shipping address left on the guest waitlist or the LX-06 Gratitude waitlist. A place on a list is a note. It is not a booking, a reservation, a membership, or a purchase. The gift is not for sale.

Who carries the note

The forms are delivered by EmailJS to info@luxemiamiaesthetics.com. EmailJS also sends you a short confirmation at the address you entered. EmailJS receives what you submit so those two notes can be sent. We do not run a shop, a payment page, or an advertising pixel on this site. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your browser

After a note goes through, this site can remember that success in the same browser session, so the confirmation can show again if you return before the session ends. That memory stays on your device. It is not an advertising cookie.

How long

We keep a note while it is still useful for the waitlist or the question you asked. Write to us if you want it deleted or corrected, and we will do that from the inbox we control.

Age

The LX-06 waitlist asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older. This site is not meant for children.

This page

If this notice changes, the date at the top will change with it. The rules for the site itself are in the terms.
