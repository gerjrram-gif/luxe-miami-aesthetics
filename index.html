The approach

Most med spas open first and figure out the standards later. I’m doing the opposite.

I’m in nursing school now so that when Luxe Miami Aesthetics opens, every treatment will be done by a licensed registered nurse working under a physician’s standing orders — the way it is supposed to be done in Florida.

Until then, this is the place to follow the build, learn what good aesthetics actually looks like, and be first in line when we open.

What’s coming

Neuromodulators — Botox and similar
Dermal fillers
Facial treatments and skin care protocols
Consultations focused on natural, balanced results

All services will be available only after licensure and formal injector training, and only under physician supervision.

Why the wait

Florida requires a medical license to inject. I chose the registered nurse path because it is the fastest legitimate route to do this under a doctor’s supervision, and because the clinical foundation matters.

The next two years are for school, hands-on injector training, and building the relationships and systems so the first day we open is already at a high standard.
