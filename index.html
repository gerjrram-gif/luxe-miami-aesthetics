Why the wait

Florida requires a medical license to inject. I chose nursing school because it is the fastest legitimate route to practice under a Physician, and because the clinical foundation matters.

The next two years are for school, hands-on injector training, and building the relationships and systems so the first day we open is already at a high standard.
