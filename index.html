FAQ

When do you open?

Luxe Miami Aesthetics is taking shape. Neither door is open. Treatments wait on RN licensure, injector training, and a physician.

Are you licensed yet?

Not yet. Mateo is in nursing school. He is not a physician. Treatments will be offered only after full licensure and under a physician.

Where will you be located?

Coral Gables and Palm Beach. Neither door is open.

Can I buy a franchise now?

No. A franchise is not being offered. The Palm Beach office is a future Luxe place, not a clinic for sale. Facials, peels, microneedling, and laser hair removal are the members-only services planned once a door can open. Injectables come only after RN licensure, injector training, and a physician. Nothing is served today. The list is not a reservation.

Can I join The Atelier now?

No. The Atelier is a future members-only circle, not a public shop. It is not open. No fee and no rate are published. The waitlist is a note for when a consultation can be offered.
