1. Real photos only

Every photo used on our website, Instagram, Facebook, email, ads, and marketing must be a real photograph taken by Luxe Miami Aesthetics or our team.

We do not use AI-generated images for brand, product, spa, or client visuals.

Traditional photo edits are fine (crop, color, brightness, dust removal). Generative fill, AI “product invent,” face swaps, and synthetic packshots are not.

2. Why

  • Meta may label posts AI info / AI content when it detects AI signals (C2PA / IPTC metadata, watermarks, and related tools).
  • We are building a medical aesthetics practice. Client trust depends on honest imagery.
  • Our brand chooses quality and authenticity over volume or synthetic shortcuts.

3. Client photo consent

Before publishing any client photo (especially before/after):

  1. Get written consent (see Photo Consent Form).
  2. Store the signed form with the client file.
  3. Honor withdrawal requests for future posts (prior posts may remain if published under valid consent unless required otherwise).

4. Before / after standards

  • Same lighting, camera height, angle, framing, and distance when practical.
  • Same makeup / hair state noted when relevant.
  • No edits that change clinical appearance (reshape, erase lines, fake swell reduction).
  • Label before and after clearly when shown together.

5. Pre-publish checklist

  • Real photo taken by our team (not AI-generated)?
  • Written consent on file if a client is identifiable?
  • Before/after consistent and labeled?
  • Caption honest — no outcome guarantees?
  • Ready for site / Instagram / Facebook?
