The rules of this page.

These terms cover luxemiamiaesthetics.com while Luxe Miami Aesthetics is taking shape. Updated November 4, 2026. How a note is handled is in the privacy notice.

Nothing is for sale

There is no shop on this site. Nothing is offered for purchase, and no payment is taken. A form does not reserve a product, a membership, a time, or a place. “Notify me” asks for first word about a piece still taking shape. It is not an order.

The guest waitlist

The guest waitlist is a way to stay close to the practice: the standards, and first word when a consultation can actually be offered. A shipping address on that form is for a complimentary gift, not a purchase. Joining does not book a visit. It does not hold a place in a treatment room. The doors are not open.

LX-06 and waitlist priority

The LX-06 Gratitude card and seal set has a waitlist, not a sale. Joining is not a purchase and not a donation. A shipping address on that form is for a complimentary gift, not a purchase of the set.

A place on that list is a request for first word. It may also mean waitlist priority only for a future injectable consult at Spa 1 in Coral Gables, if that priority is later offered. It is not a medical offer, a booking, or a prepaid service, and it has no cash value. No consult or treatment date is guaranteed, and Spa 1 may not open. A note creates no obligation to provide treatment. Any treatment would follow a clinical evaluation and happen only if clinically appropriate, and only after licensure, injector training, and a physician are in place. One place on the list, one person.

A future studio

A note about a future studio is interest only. It is not a franchise offer. No fee, territory, or disclosure is published here. Nothing is reserved, and no address is for sale. Palm Beach and Coral Gables are future places for this practice. They are not clinics you can buy from this page.

No treatment today

Luxe Miami Aesthetics does not provide medical services today. The founder is in nursing school and is not a physician. Injectables, if they are ever offered, begin only after RN licensure and formal injector training, under a physician. Words on this site are not medical advice, and they are not a promise that a door will open on a given day.

Age

The LX-06 waitlist is for people who are 18 or older.
